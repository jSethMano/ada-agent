## Summary

Use this guide when new messages do not appear, outgoing messages remain pending, or your approved email application displays outdated mailbox information. The issue may involve a connection problem, the wrong mailbox, a stuck message, or a synchronization error.

## Steps

1. Confirm that your device has a working internet or company network connection.
2. Verify that you are viewing the intended work account, inbox, and folder. Check the spam or junk folder for expected messages.
3. Use the application's normal refresh or synchronization function and allow time for messages to update.
4. If a message is stuck in the outbox, check whether its attachment is too large or its recipient address may be incorrect.
5. Review any mailbox capacity warning shown by the approved email application.

## If that didn't work

- Close and reopen the email application. If the issue continues, restart your device after saving other work.
- If available, check the same mailbox using another company-approved device or email access method.
- Ask the IT helpdesk whether there is a known email service outage.

Do not repeatedly send the same message without checking whether it was already delivered. Do not forward confidential work messages to a personal email account as a workaround.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if messages remain missing, outgoing email repeatedly fails, or the issue persists across approved devices. Include the approximate time the problem began, whether sending or receiving is affected, and any error messages. Do not attach confidential messages unless the approved support process requires them.
