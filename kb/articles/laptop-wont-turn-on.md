## Summary

Use this guide when your company laptop does not appear to power on, shows no signs of charging, or displays a blank screen during startup. The device may have no visible response to the power button, or it may start briefly before shutting down.

## Steps

1. Confirm that the wall outlet is working by checking it with another suitable device or using another approved outlet.
2. Check that the approved power adapter is firmly connected to the laptop and outlet. Look for obvious damage to the cable or plug.
3. If the battery may be depleted, connect the approved charger and leave the laptop charging for approximately 15 minutes before trying to start it again.
4. Look for power indicators, keyboard lights, or fan activity. If the laptop appears to be running, adjust screen brightness and check whether an external display is masking the issue.
5. Remove nonessential USB devices, docks, and other peripherals, then try starting the laptop with its normal power connection.

## If that didn't work

- If the laptop responds but remains stuck on a screen, follow its approved normal restart procedure.
- Check the IT helpdesk's support guidance using another available device.
- Record whether the device shows indicator lights, charging activity, or other signs of power.

Do not open the laptop casing, remove the internal battery, or attempt electrical repairs.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the device still will not start, the charger or battery appears damaged, or the laptop repeatedly shuts down. Report the device asset number if available, the symptoms, and any visible indicator lights.
