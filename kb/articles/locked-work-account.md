## Summary

Use this guide when your work account becomes temporarily locked after repeated unsuccessful sign-in attempts or when a service reports that access has been blocked. You may be unable to access company services even when attempting to enter your usual password.

## Steps

1. Stop repeatedly submitting your password, as additional attempts may extend the lockout or trigger further security controls.
2. Confirm that you are using the correct work account rather than a personal account or an old username.
3. If you suspect the password is incorrect, use the company's approved password recovery process instead of guessing.
4. If the lockout message specifies a waiting period, allow that period to pass before attempting to sign in again.

## If that didn't work

- If you recently changed your password, check whether another approved device is still using the old password. Update it through the application's normal sign-in process when prompted.
- Ask the IT helpdesk for the official account recovery instructions if self-service recovery is unavailable.
- Note whether the lockout affects one service or multiple company services.

Do not bypass account protections, use another employee's account, or create an unauthorized replacement account.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the account remains locked after the indicated waiting period, no self-service recovery option is available, or you suspect someone else has attempted to access your account. Provide your work username, the affected service, and the displayed error message.
