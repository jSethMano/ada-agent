## Summary

Use this guide when you cannot hear sound through your speakers or headset, or when other participants cannot hear you during an online meeting. The problem may involve muted controls, an incorrect audio device, a disconnected headset, or application permissions.

## Steps

1. If you use a wired headset, make sure its cable is securely connected. For wireless equipment, confirm that it is powered on and connected to your device.
2. Increase the speaker volume and verify that the headset, device, and meeting controls are not muted.
3. Open the meeting application's audio settings and select the intended speaker and microphone from the available options.
4. Use the application's built-in speaker and microphone test, if available. Speak normally and check whether the microphone input indicator responds.
5. Review the normal application or browser permissions available to your account and ensure microphone access is allowed.

## If that didn't work

- Close other applications that may be using the microphone, then reopen the meeting application.
- Disconnect and reconnect the headset, or restart the device after saving your work.
- Check whether the audio problem occurs in multiple applications or only one.

Do not install unofficial audio drivers or change restricted device policies.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the microphone or speakers remain unavailable across multiple applications, your device does not recognize an approved headset, or audio problems affect several employees. Include your device type, headset connection type, affected application, and the results of your audio test.
