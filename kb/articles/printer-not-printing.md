## Summary

Use this guide when a document does not print on an approved office printer or when the printer appears offline. Documents may remain in the print queue, or printed pages may be blank or incomplete.

## Steps

1. Open the print dialog and confirm that you selected the correct approved office printer rather than a PDF destination or an unavailable printer.
2. Check the printer's display for messages about missing paper, an open tray, or a paper jam.
3. Confirm that paper is loaded and check for visible toner or ink warnings, where applicable.
4. If the printer connects directly to your device, check that its cable is securely attached. For a shared network printer, confirm that your computer is connected to the office network.
5. Open the standard printer settings available to your account. If an earlier document is stuck, cancel your own pending job if that option is available.
6. Print the document again and check whether the problem affects one document or all print jobs.

## If that didn't work

- Restart the computer and retry printing after it reconnects to the printer.
- Ask the IT helpdesk whether there is a known printer outage.
- Record any persistent printer display error and whether other users are also unable to print.

Do not open printer panels, remove internal parts, or install unofficial printer drivers.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the printer remains unavailable, several employees cannot print, an error requires maintenance, or repeated attempts produce blank or incomplete pages. Include the printer's location, its displayed error, and whether the problem affects one document or every print job.
