## Summary

Use this guide when your company computer responds slowly, takes longer than usual to open applications, or frequently becomes temporarily unresponsive. Performance issues may appear when switching between windows, using work applications, or performing routine tasks.

## Steps

1. Save open documents and confirm that important work is backed up to an approved company location.
2. Close applications and browser tabs that you no longer need, particularly those consuming substantial memory.
3. Use the normal storage settings available to your account to check whether the disk is nearly full.
4. Remove only unnecessary personal downloads or temporary files that you are authorized to delete.
5. If performance becomes slow immediately after sign-in, note which applications start automatically. Do not disable security tools or required company software.
6. Follow approved update notifications available to your account, and allow required updates to complete rather than forcing the device to shut down.

## If that didn't work

- Save your work, close your applications, and restart the computer through its standard power menu.
- Observe whether the slowdown occurs during video meetings, when opening a particular application, or throughout the workday.
- Record whether the computer freezes repeatedly, overheats, or displays an error.

Avoid downloading third-party cleanup tools or deleting company files to free space.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the computer remains slow after restarting, freezes repeatedly, overheats, or cannot run the applications needed for work. Include when the problem began, which applications are affected, any error messages, and whether the slowdown is constant or intermittent.
