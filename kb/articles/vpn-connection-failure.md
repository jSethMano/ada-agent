## Summary

Use this guide when you cannot establish a company VPN connection while working remotely or when an existing connection disconnects unexpectedly. Symptoms may include connection timeouts, repeated failures, or inaccessible company resources even when the VPN appears connected.

## Steps

1. Open a familiar public website to confirm that your home or other approved network has a working internet connection.
2. Check the company's published remote-work instructions to determine whether the VPN is required for the resource you are accessing.
3. Disconnect from the VPN, wait a few seconds, and reconnect using the approved VPN application and sign-in method.

## If that didn't work

- Confirm that you are using your current work account and that your password has not expired. Never share your password with support personnel.
- Save your work, restart your laptop, and attempt another VPN connection.
- Ask the IT helpdesk whether there is a known VPN outage.

Do not disable security software, install another VPN application, or change company network settings to bypass the connection problem.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if the VPN continues to fail, your account is rejected despite correct credentials, or several remote employees experience the same issue. Include the error message, approximate time of failure, whether your regular internet connection works, and whether the problem started after a password change.
