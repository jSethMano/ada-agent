## Summary

Use this guide when your computer cannot connect to the company Wi-Fi, repeatedly disconnects, or reports that it is connected without internet access. The network may be missing from the available networks list, or the wireless signal may be weak.

## Steps

1. Make sure Wi-Fi is enabled on your device and airplane mode is turned off.
2. Move closer to the office wireless access point or to an area where the Wi-Fi signal is stronger.
3. Disconnect from the company Wi-Fi, wait a few seconds, and reconnect using your usual approved access method.
4. Check whether another approved device can connect to the same network to help determine whether the problem affects one device or multiple users.

## If that didn't work

- Save your work, restart your computer, and try connecting again.
- Ask the IT helpdesk whether there is a known network outage.
- Note whether the company Wi-Fi network is missing entirely or appears but fails to connect.

## When to file a ticket

File a ticket if you still cannot connect after these checks, if the company Wi-Fi remains unavailable on your device, or if multiple employees are affected. Include your work location, device type, the time the issue began, and any displayed error messages.
